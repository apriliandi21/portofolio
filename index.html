About Me

Get to Know Me.

A short introduction — who I am, what drives me, and where I'm heading.

Hi! I'm Dian, an IT student who genuinely enjoys figuring out how technology works — and then building something useful with it. What started as curiosity about computers grew into a real interest in programming and system development: writing code, solving problems, and turning ideas into working digital products.

Lately, I've been especially drawn to Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — how machines can learn from data, recognize patterns, and help people make better decisions. Fields like computer vision fascinate me because they let software actually "see" the world.

I also enjoy teaching and sharing what I've learned with others. I have experience helping others understand the material — explaining concepts in a simple way — even though I'm still in the learning stage myself, and teaching is one of the ways I learn best.

I'm still early in my journey, and that's exactly what motivates me: there's always a new concept to learn, a new tool to try, and a better way to build something. My goal is simple — keep learning, keep building, and grow into a developer who creates technology that genuinely helps people.
